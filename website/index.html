For Windows, macOS and Linux · open source

A free desktop client for the Stremio addon ecosystem.

Browse catalogs, keep your place and watch on a real player, all from the addons you choose. Open source, built in Rust, and speaking the open Stremio addon protocol.

What Zeiler is, and isn’t

Zeiler hosts no media, indexes no media, and ships no content addon. You bring your own addons; Zeiler is just the boat.

Out of the box it only knows a few built-in metadata and subtitle addons — Cinemeta, Anime Kitsu and OpenSubtitles v3 — so there is something to browse. None of them serves streams, and each can be turned off.

A real player

Everything plays on Zeiler’s own player screen: play and pause, seeking, volume, audio and subtitle tracks, and fullscreen, all from the keyboard too.

  • Windows: video plays right inside the Zeiler window. Nothing else to install.
  • macOS and Linux: video plays in an mpv window that Zeiler drives with the same controls. Built-in playback for macOS comes next.
  • A built-in torrent engine and mpv-based playback: no external streaming server, browser or Node runtime.

Your library, and Continue Watching

Zeiler remembers what you watch, and picks up where you stopped.

  • Continue Watching on Home and in My Library. Finish an episode and it moves on to the next one.
  • My Library has three tabs: Library (with sorting), Continue Watching and History.
  • Watched marks per episode, per season or for the whole title, and Resume from 12:34 when you come back.
  • Progress is saved by Zeiler itself, in a format compatible with Stremio’s own library.

Subtitles and audio, your way

  • Subtitles from the video, from your subtitle addons and from OpenSubtitles, picked automatically in your preferred languages.
  • Load a subtitle file from your computer, or drop one on the window.
  • Adjust subtitle delay, size and style, and audio delay and speed. Zeiler remembers your choices per show.
  • Subtitles in older encodings are converted automatically, and you can pick another encoding if the text still looks wrong.

Bring your own addons

Paste any addon’s manifest URL on the Addons page. Zeiler ships no stream or content addon, and endorses none.

  • The built-ins only ever provide metadata or subtitles, never streams: Cinemeta for catalogs, details and episode lists, Anime Kitsu for anime, and OpenSubtitles v3 for subtitles.
  • Each built-in can be switched off in Settings → Content, which also says what each one sends where.

Keeps itself up to date

Zeiler checks for new versions on its own and downloads them in the background. An update installs the next time you open Zeiler, never in the middle of a film, or right away if you ask. Every update is signed, and Zeiler checks the signature before it installs anything.

Zeiler is free software under the GPL-3.0. Its addon client, streaming engine and playback are Rust crates, with a thin TypeScript interface in a Tauri 2 shell. Read the source on GitHub.

Playback on each system

Where videos play is a setting: built in where Zeiler has it, or in your own mpv window.

  • Windows 10 and 11

    Built in

    Video plays inside the Zeiler window, with libmpv shipped alongside. If you prefer your own mpv window, that’s a setting.

  • macOS 12 and later

    built-in playback

    Video plays inside the Zeiler window, with libmpv shipped inside the app. If you prefer your own mpv window, install mpv (brew install mpv) and choose it in Settings.

  • Linux

    mpv window

    The .deb pulls in mpv for you. With the .rpm or the AppImage, install mpv from your distro.

Questions

Does Zeiler come with anything to watch?

No. Zeiler hosts no media, indexes no media and ships no content addon. It is a client for the open Stremio addon protocol: what you can watch depends entirely on the addons you add.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I open it?

The builds aren’t code-signed or notarized yet. On Windows, if SmartScreen warns, choose More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click Zeiler → Open the first time, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Do I need mpv?

On Windows, no: playback is built in. On macOS and Linux, yes, for now: Zeiler finds mpv in the usual places, or you can set its path in Settings.

I used Sailor. What happens to it?

Zeiler was called Sailor up to version 0.1.2. Sailor installs update to Zeiler on their own and keep your addons and settings. On macOS the updated app keeps the name Sailor.app: rename it to Zeiler.app, or replace it with the new download.

Is Zeiler made by Stremio?

No. Zeiler is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Stremio (Smart Code Ltd). It speaks the same open addon protocol.
